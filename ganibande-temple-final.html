A place to pause, pray and find peace

Sri Venkateshwara Swamy Temple, also known locally as Sri Venkataramana Swamy Temple, is in Ganibande near Rayalpad, Srinivaspur Taluk, Kolar District, Karnataka.

This website shares visitor information, darshan timings, local traditions, contact details and function-hall booking enquiries for devotees.

Devotee notice: Timings and historical stories shown here are based on the information currently available and should be confirmed with temple representatives before travel or booking.

Tradition & local stories

Temple History & Special Features

The following details are shared in local accounts and community posts; please verify them with temple elders or management before treating them as official historical records.

Ancient Temple Tradition

Local accounts describe the Sri Venkataramana Swamy Temple as an ancient place of worship, believed by some to date back around 800 years and associated with the Vijayanagara period (14th–15th century). This date has not been independently verified here.

The Peepal / Ravi Tree

Local stories describe a sacred Peepal (Ravi) tree growing through the roof of the temple mandapam. Visitors should follow temple guidance around this sacred feature.

Local Legend of Ganibande

A local explanation links the name “Gavi Banda” with Kannada words for cave and rock, and tells of a nearby cave associated with Gautama Muni’s penance. This is presented as local legend, not a verified historical fact.
