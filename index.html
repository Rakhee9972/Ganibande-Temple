🙏 Welcome to the Temple

Sri Venkateshwara Swamy Temple, Ganibande, welcomes devotees to seek the blessings of Lord Sri Venkateshwara Swamy. Devotees are invited to visit the temple, participate in prayers and experience the peaceful spiritual atmosphere.
