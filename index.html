🙏 Welcome to Ganibande

Welcome to Sri Venkateshwara Swamy Temple, Ganibande. Devotees visit this sacred temple to offer prayers and seek the blessings of Lord Venkateshwara.

May Lord Venkateshwara bless all devotees with peace, health and happiness.

📍 Temple Location

Sri Venkateshwara Swamy Temple, Ganibande, Yaramvaripalli Post, Rayalpad Hobli, Srinivaspur Taluk, Kolar District, Karnataka, India.

🙏 Today's Shlokam

ॐ नमो वेङ्कटेशाय नमः ॥

English Meaning
